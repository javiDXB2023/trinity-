## The Concept

A first-person VR metaverse where players don virtual sunglasses and enter a new universe. Complete missions, earn crypto, unlock real-world value.

## Visual Identity

- **Aesthetic**: Minimal terminal / black & white
- **Logo**: The Hand of Adam meets AI - human and machine reaching toward each other
- **Colors**: Trinity of AI (OpenAI Green | Claude Orange | Gemini Blue)
